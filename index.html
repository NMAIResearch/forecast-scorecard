Receipts. Arithmetic and spans use only observations whose current row and source digests carry a supported receipt. A candidate in CANDIDATE_OBSERVATIONS.json enters spans, the like-for-like comparison and declared comparisons, as a registered observation does, only when CANDIDATE_SUPPORT_REVIEW.json holds a supported receipt bound to its current row and to the candidate source list; admitted lists the candidates that do.

Field definitions
coverage
total_facility_IT_plus_overhead: total consumption including facility overhead, as distinct from IT-only; data_centres_unspecified: the facility boundary is not fully stated, and stated workload or developer dimensions are not a complete boundary. it_load: IT demand only, excluding facility overhead. new_data_centres_unspecified: the figure covers new data centres only, and its facility boundary is not fully stated.
crypto scope
excluded_inferred: the exclusion is stated for a related calculation and applied to this series by inference.
metric
data_centre_capacity_demand: power demand stated in GW; data_centre_capacity: capacity stated in GW without saying whether installed or demanded; installed_capacity: capacity stated as expected online; annual_electricity: energy in TWh.
shape
point: a single stated value; scenario_range: stated scenario bounds with an optional reference point; upper_bound: only an upper value is stated, so low and point are null. lower_bound: only a lower value is stated, so high and point are null; whether the bound is inclusive is stated in the qualification.
superseded by
A later vintage of the same publisher series and quantity, not necessarily the immediately following one.
unit
TWh: annual energy; GW: power or capacity; percent_growth: growth relative to base_year. A value the source states in another unit of the same quantity, such as MW, is recorded after an exact conversion, which the qualification states.
value basis
Whether the registered figure is a table value, a text value or a chart label.
Scope limits
  • Convenience sample of existing named institutions; not a census or forecast-accuracy test.
  • Source matching confirms what publishers state, not that the forecasts are true.
  • No underlying provider model has been reconstructed in this revision.
  • Unresolved sources are retained separately and excluded from calculated envelopes.
  • Recorded-value spans list superseded same-publisher vintages separately and retain scope, scenario and shape mismatches; no pooled consensus, uplift or model ranking is calculated.
  • No metres, site inspections or realised national US data-centre outcome series were collected.
  • A document available publicly does not prove that its complete input data and executable model are public.
  • Disclosed input links among registered providers: the IEA model relies on a bottom-up approach developed by LBNL (S02, Annex A methodology); Goldman Sachs uses a 451 Research data set (S09); Deloitte's November 2024 estimate cites Goldman Sachs and EPRI among its sources (S13, endnote 1), which predates their later registered observations; Deloitte's April 2025 estimate names LBNL's 2024 data-centre energy report among its inputs (S20, endnote 1), an earlier report than the registered LBNL 2025 update (S03). Agreement among linked providers cannot automatically be counted as independent confirmation; the exact shared inputs and vintages are not established.
  • Support review receipts bind each observation and the complete source catalogue by digest. Changing a claim field or bound evidence makes its receipt stale; the status becomes review stale and arithmetic is refused. The maker_assessment field is excluded from the claim digest. Only observations with a current supported receipt enter arithmetic or spans.
  • A currency sweep on 8 October 2026 searched each registered publisher for newer editions; it is a bounded search, not proof that no newer forecast exists.
